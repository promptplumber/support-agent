# Inviting team members

Invite people from Settings > Members > Invite. Enter their email and pick a role. They receive an invite link valid for 7 days. Pending invites count toward your plan's member limit only after they accept. On the free Starter plan you can have up to 3 members total including yourself. To invite in bulk, paste multiple emails separated by commas. You can revoke a pending invite from the same page.
