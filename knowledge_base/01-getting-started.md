# Getting started with Nimbus

Nimbus is a team task and time-tracking app. To get started, sign up at app.nimbus.example with a work email, verify the email, then create your first workspace. A workspace holds your projects, tasks, and team members. After creating a workspace you are the workspace Owner. Invite teammates from Settings > Members. New workspaces start on the free Starter plan, which supports up to 3 members and 2 active projects. You can create your first project from the Projects tab by clicking New Project.
