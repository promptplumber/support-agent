# Roles and permissions

Nimbus has four roles. Owner: full control including billing and deleting the workspace; there is exactly one Owner. Admin: manage members, projects, and settings, but not billing unless granted. Member: create and work on tasks in projects they belong to. Guest: view and comment only, and only in projects they are added to. Advanced per-project permissions are available on the Business plan. Change a person's role from Settings > Members.
