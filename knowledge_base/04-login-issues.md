# Fixing login problems

If you cannot log in, first confirm you are using the correct email. Common causes are a caps-lock password, a browser autofilling an old password, or an account that uses SSO. After five failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes as a security measure. If you see 'account not found', you may be trying to log into the wrong region; Nimbus has separate EU and US logins. Clearing cookies for app.nimbus.example fixes most stuck-login problems.
