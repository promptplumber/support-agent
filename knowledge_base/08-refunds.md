# Refund policy

Nimbus offers a full refund within 14 days of a new paid subscription or an upgrade charge. After 14 days, charges are non-refundable, but you can cancel to stop future charges. Prorated upgrade charges follow the same 14-day window. Refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. To request a refund, contact support with the invoice number from Settings > Billing > Invoices.
