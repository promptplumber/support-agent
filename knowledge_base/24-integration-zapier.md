# Zapier and webhooks

Automate Nimbus with Zapier or raw webhooks (Team and Business plans). Triggers include task created, task completed, and time entry added; actions include create task and add time entry. Generate a webhook signing secret in Settings > Integrations > Webhooks and verify the signature on your endpoint. Webhooks retry failed deliveries for up to 24 hours. Use these to connect Nimbus to tools we do not integrate with directly.
