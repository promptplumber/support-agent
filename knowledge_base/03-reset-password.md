# Resetting your password

To reset your password, go to the login screen and click Forgot password. Enter your account email and we send a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. Open the link and choose a new password of at least 10 characters with one number. If the link expired, request a new one. Accounts created with Google or Microsoft SSO do not have a Nimbus password; sign in through that provider instead. Reset links can only be used once.
