# Invoices and receipts

Every payment generates an invoice you can download from Settings > Billing > Invoices as a PDF. Invoices list the plan, the number of billed members, the period, and any proration. To add a company name, tax ID, or billing address to future invoices, edit the billing details on the same page before your next charge; invoices already issued cannot be edited. Only workspace Owners and Admins can see billing.
