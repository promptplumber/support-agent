# Plans and pricing

Nimbus has three plans. Starter is free: up to 3 members, 2 active projects, 30-day history. Team is 9 USD per member per month: unlimited projects, unlimited history, integrations, and reports. Business is 18 USD per member per month: everything in Team plus SSO, advanced permissions, and audit logs. Annual billing gives two months free. Prices are per member and are billed for the members active on your workspace during the billing period.
